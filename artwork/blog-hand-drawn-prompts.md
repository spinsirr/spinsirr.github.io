# Blog hand-drawn illustrations

These are the editable generation briefs for the `hand-drawn.webp` images in `public/blog/<slug>/`. Each image is a conceptual illustration, not a screenshot or evidence figure. Keep the blog's original charts and cited figures for measured claims.

Shared direction: light ink and watercolor on warm cream paper; landscape 3:2; charcoal lines with restrained teal, blue, and amber washes; simple shapes and generous whitespace; no text, numerals, logos, people, or fake UI. Render at blog width and export an optimized WebP at 1400 px.
